# GoReady Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

## 1. Introduction

GoReady (“the App”) is designed to help users manage disaster preparedness information, emergency supply inventories, and related emergency response reference materials.

We respect your privacy and design the App according to the principle of data minimization. This Privacy Policy explains how the App stores, uses, and transmits information.

## 2. User-Created and Stored Data

The App may store the following information in its app-specific storage on the user’s device:

- Emergency bag information
- Item names, quantities, notes, brands, purchase dates, places of purchase, prices, expiration dates, and warranty dates
- User-created tags and category names
- Emergency contact names, relationships, telephone numbers, and notes
- Emergency contact card information, including:
  - Name
  - Telephone number
  - Emergency meeting locations
- Images selected by the user and associated with emergency bags, items, or emergency contact cards

This information is primarily stored in the App’s local, app-specific storage.

GoReady currently does not provide a developer-operated account system or cloud synchronization service for emergency preparedness data. The App does not upload the database or associated images described above to servers operated by the GoReady developer.

## 3. Operating System Backups and Device Transfer

Depending on the user’s device, operating system version, and backup settings, the App’s local data may be backed up or restored through Apple iCloud Backup, Android Auto Backup, or operating-system device transfer features.

These backups are processed by Apple, Google, or the device manufacturer under their respective terms and privacy policies. They are not cloud synchronization services provided by GoReady.

Users can manage available backup options through their device settings.

## 4. Permissions and Device Features

The App may request the following permissions when required by a feature.

### Photo Access

When the user selects an image for an emergency bag, item, or emergency contact card, the App may request access to the selected photo.

The selected image is copied into the App’s app-specific storage.

### Notifications

The App may request notification permission to provide disaster preparedness reminders, such as item expiration reminders.

These reminders are handled through the device’s notification system.

### Location

When the user chooses to include their current location in a safety check-in message, the App requests foreground location permission and obtains the device’s current latitude and longitude.

The location is used to prepare the message requested by the user. The App does not continuously track the user’s location and does not send this location to servers operated by the GoReady developer.

Users may decline these permissions or change them at any time through their device settings.

## 5. Information Shared at the User’s Direction

When the user uses the calling or safety check-in messaging feature, the App may provide a telephone number, name, and—if specifically selected by the user—the user’s current location to the device’s phone or messaging application.

The user reviews and confirms the message through the system messaging interface before sending it. After the message is sent, the information may be processed by the user’s telecommunications provider, messaging service, and the recipient.

When the user opens a bug report or feedback form, the App directs the user to a form provided through Google Forms. To assist with troubleshooting, the form link may be prefilled with the following technical information:

- Device platform
- Operating system version
- App version and build number
- Device time zone, for bug reports only

Information that the user enters and submits through the form is processed by Google and the GoReady maintenance team for the stated purpose of the form.

Users should not include emergency contact card information, telephone numbers, precise location data, or other unnecessary sensitive information in these forms.

GoReady does not sell user data.

## 6. Error Monitoring and Diagnostic Data

To maintain the stability of the App, we use Sentry for error and crash monitoring in production and preview versions.

Diagnostic data that may be transmitted includes:

- Error types, error codes, and technical stack traces
- App version, runtime environment, and update identifiers
- Technical information such as operating system, device platform, language, and time zone
- A randomly generated installation identifier
- Database schema version
- Types of features or actions involved when an error occurs

We have disabled Sentry's default transmission of personally identifiable information and implemented data filtering mechanisms to reduce the risk of unintentionally transmitting names, phone numbers, meeting locations, notes, or other sensitive information.

Diagnostic data is processed by Sentry in accordance with its privacy and security policies and is used solely for error analysis, system security, and service improvement.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Users can edit or delete data they have created within the App.

When data is deleted, the App removes the corresponding local data. However, copies previously created by an operating-system backup may continue to exist under the backup retention practices of Apple, Google, or the device manufacturer. Such data may reappear if an earlier device backup is restored.

Uninstalling the App will generally remove data from the App’s app-specific storage on the current device. It may not automatically delete:

- Existing cloud or device backups
- Messages that have already been sent
- Bug reports or feedback that has already been submitted

Diagnostic information and form submissions are retained only for the period required by the relevant service provider and the GoReady maintenance team for the purposes described in this policy.

## 8. Third-Party and System Services

The App may use or interact with the following third-party or operating-system services:

- System backup and device transfer services provided by Apple or Google
- Sentry error-monitoring services
- Google Forms for bug reports and feedback
- Device phone, messaging, notification, photo-selection, and location services

These services may process information under their own privacy policies.

## 9. Children’s Privacy

GoReady is not specifically designed for children who are below the age at which they may independently consent under applicable law.

If a parent or legal guardian allows a child to use the App, they should assist the child in avoiding the entry or external transmission of unnecessary sensitive personal information.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the App’s features, legal requirements, or third-party services.

When this policy is updated, we will revise the “Last updated” date shown above.

If a change materially affects how information is collected, used, or shared, we may provide an additional notice within the App or through an appropriate publication channel.
