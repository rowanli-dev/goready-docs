GoReady Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 15, 2026

1. Introduction

GoReady (“the App”) is designed to help users manage emergency preparedness information, disaster supply lists, and related emergency reference data.

We value your privacy and aim to minimize data collection whenever possible.

2. Information Stored by the App

The App may store information entered by the user, including:

* Emergency preparedness bag information
* Item names, quantities, notes, brands, purchase dates, purchase sources, prices, and warranty periods
* Emergency contact information
* Emergency card information, such as:
  * Name
  * Birthday
  * Phone number
  * Blood type
  * Allergy information
  * Medication notes
  * Chronic condition notes
  * Organization or unit information
  * Emergency gathering location
* User-created labels and tag names related to preparedness items
* User-selected images related to preparedness bags, items, or emergency cards

Most data is stored locally on the user's device.

3. Local Storage

Most user data is stored locally on the user’s device.

At this time, GoReady does not provide automatic cloud synchronization for emergency card information or preparedness data.

4. Data Sharing

GoReady does not sell user data.

GoReady does not share personal emergency card information with third parties unless the user explicitly initiates a future sharing feature.

If sharing features are introduced in future versions, users will be informed before any data is shared.

5. Analytics and Error Monitoring

The App may use third-party services for application stability and error monitoring, such as crash reporting tools.

We aim to avoid sending sensitive emergency card information, including medical-related notes, through these monitoring systems.

6. Notifications

GoReady may request permission to send notifications related to preparedness reminders, such as item expiration reminders.

Notification permissions can be managed through the device settings.

7. User Control and Data Deletion

Users may edit or delete their data directly within the App at any time.

Removing the App from the device may also remove locally stored App data, depending on the operating system behavior.

8. Children’s Privacy

GoReady is not directed toward children under the age required by applicable laws for independent consent.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

Any updates will be reflected by revising the “Last updated” date above.
